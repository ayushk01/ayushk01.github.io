## Template

This file is a template, not a live case study. It still creates a `/work/side-project/` route — feel free to either:

1. Delete this file once you have real case studies you'd rather show.
2. Or replace its contents with a future side-project writeup and rename to something like `content/work/my-cool-project.mdx`.

Structure to follow:

- **The product** — 1 short paragraph: what it is, who it's for, why it exists.
- **What I built / owned** — bulleted list of features and decisions.
- **A few hard things** — 2–4 paragraphs of real engineering reasoning: a bug you fought, an architecture call, a perf win. This is the part recruiters actually read.
- **Stack notes** — tech list.
