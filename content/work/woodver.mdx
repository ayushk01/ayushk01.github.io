## The product

WoodVer is the Indian arm of an Italian wood-coatings manufacturer (Verinlegno). I built the original version of their loyalty-rewards Android app — sign up, accrue points on purchases, redeem against products.

> The app's current Play Store version has since been replaced by a different build commissioned by the client. The listing remains live: [Google Play — WoodVer Reward](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.WoodverCoatingBasiq360.app).

## What I built

- Authentication and onboarding flows.
- A points wallet, with transaction history.
- A catalog of redeemable products and rewards.
- A simple admin-friendly backend for the client to update the catalog.

## Stack notes

- **Mobile:** Flutter / Dart for a single Android target.
- **Backend / data:** Firebase (Auth + Firestore + Cloud Functions for the redemption flow).
- **Distribution:** Google Play, signed and submitted by me.
